Here are the basics.

What is OkayDev, and who is it for?

OkayDev is a community and portfolio platform for creative developers, designers, and the studios that hire them. Build a profile, show off your work, and connect with people who make things.

Do I have to pay to get my work seen?

No. Your profile and listed projects are visible to the community on the free plan, and free members' projects can be hand-picked for the Gallery.

PRO makes discovery automatic. New projects you create as a PRO member are included in the Gallery, and you appear in the Directory, where people can find you by skills, availability, and location.

What makes Okay Social different?

A chronological feed for sharing work, ideas, links, code, and works in progress. Posts appear in order from people you follow. Membership never changes their position. No ads, no ranking, no reach games.

How does Lifetime work?

Lifetime is one payment with no renewals. You get everything in PRO, first access to new features, a LIFETIME profile badge, and premium support. Seats are limited to 250 per account type, and the price goes up as they're claimed. Check the pricing page to see how many are left.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel PRO from Membership in your account settings and it stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Your profile and work stay put. Purchases aren't refundable, so review the plans before you buy.

Can I use OkayDev on my phone?

Yes. OkayDev works in any modern mobile browser, and you can add it to your Home Screen to use it like an app.

Once it's installed, you can turn on push notifications and get alerts on your phone for things like new followers, likes, comments, and mentions. You choose which types to receive in your notification settings.
